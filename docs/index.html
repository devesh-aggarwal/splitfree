SSplitFree

Split anything, fairly

Track shared expenses with friends, flatmates and travel companions. Everyone's balance stays exact, down to the last cent.

Free, and that is the whole story

No adverts. No subscription. No upgrade screen, and nothing held back for a paid tier. Every feature is in the app the moment you install it.

What it does

  • Six ways to divide a bill: equally, exact amounts, percentages, shares, plus and minus adjustments, or line by line off the receipt
  • Scan a receipt and read the items straight off it
  • Debt simplification, so three people settle in two payments instead of six
  • 150+ currencies, with the exchange rate stored per expense so old totals never drift
  • Recurring expenses, CSV import and export, charts, and search that reaches into scanned receipts
  • Works completely offline

Your data

Groups stay on your phone. Sharing one with a friend is a separate, deliberate act that uploads that group so their phone can reach it. Everything you never share never leaves the device. An account is optional and the app is fully usable without one.
